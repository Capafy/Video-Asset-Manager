## Distribution requirements

- Keep these license files with the fonts in source archives, installed webapp
  bundles, and other redistributions.
- The OFL permits use, embedding, and redistribution with software, including
  commercial software; do not sell the font files on their own.
- Keep font derivatives under the OFL and follow any Reserved Font Name
  restrictions before modifying or renaming a font family.
- Do not imply endorsement by the font authors.
- A rendered video or image is not itself required to use the OFL merely because
  it contains text rendered with these fonts.
- When replacing a font, verify the new binary's actual license and update its
  provenance, checksum, and notice together.

This inventory establishes a reproducible match to the named upstream release
and records its license terms. It is not a warranty of every possible use,
third-party ownership claim, or content generated by a user. The full OFL terms
in the accompanying files govern use and redistribution.
